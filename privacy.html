感谢您使用 GK-Mixer（以下简称“本应用”）。本应用由独立开发者开发，致力于为模型喷涂、手办制作与数字色彩设计爱好者提供高效纯粹的本地色彩工具。我们高度重视并严格保护每一位用户的个人隐私权。

本政策旨在向您清晰透明地说明：本应用在数据收集、本地处理及安全保护方面的基本原则。

1. 零个人隐私数据收集

  • 无账号体系：本应用无需注册或登录账号，不收集您的真实姓名、手机号、身份证号、电子邮件地址或社交账号信息。
  • 无设备敏感权限：本应用不收集您的地理位置（GPS）、通讯录、麦克风录音、剪贴板无关内容或设备硬件序列号。

2. 图片与取色数据的本地安全处理

  • 100% 本地运算：当您使用“照片取色”功能从相册导入图片时，所有的像素采样、颜色识别、色号比对与大图渲染完全在您的设备本地内存与本地沙盒中完成。
  • 绝无云端上传：本应用没有配置外部业务服务器，绝对不会将您的任何私人照片或图像数据上传至任何云端或第三方服务器。

3. 系统权限调用说明

  • 照片库读取权限 (Photo Library - Read)：仅在您主动点击“从相册选择图片”进行色彩提取与色号比对时由系统调起，仅在本地读取您选择的单张图片。
  • 照片库写入权限 (Photo Library - Add Only)：仅在您主动点击“保存到相册”导出带有引线标注文案的色卡大图时使用。

4. 无第三方追踪与广告

  • 不追踪用户行为：本应用不包含任何第三方追踪 SDK，不请求也不使用广告标识符（IDFA），不从事跨应用、跨网站的行为跟踪，严格遵循 Apple《App 跟踪透明度》(ATT) 与 NSPrivacyTracking: false 规范。
  • 无第三方广告接入：本应用无任何开屏广告、横幅广告或插屏弹窗广告。

5. 应用内购买与订阅说明

  • 本应用提供的“终身买断”及“Pro 订阅”均通过 Apple 官方应用内购买机制（StoreKit 2）进行处理。
  • 所有的订单支付、订阅扣费、续订管理与发票均由 Apple 官方结算保障。开发者完全无法接触、收集或存储您的任何支付账号、银行卡号或支付密码。

Thank you for using GK-Mixer ("the App"). The App is created by an independent developer to provide powerful, pure, and offline color mixing and paint management tools for scale modelers, miniaturists, and digital colorists. We deeply respect and are committed to protecting your privacy.

This Privacy Policy outlines our principles regarding data collection, on-device processing, and privacy protection.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

  • No Account Required: The App does not require registration or sign-in. We do not collect names, phone numbers, email addresses, or demographic identifiers.
  • No Sensitive Identifiers: We do not track location data (GPS), contacts, microphone audio, clipboard history, or hardware identifiers.

2. 100% On-Device Photo & Color Processing

  • Local Processing: When using the eyedropper feature to sample colors from photos, all image decoding, pixel sampling, color difference matching, and annotation card rendering occur strictly on your local device.
  • No Cloud Uploads: The App does not operate external application servers. Your personal photos and palettes are never uploaded to any remote or third-party servers.

3. System Permissions

  • Photos Access (Read): Used strictly when you explicitly select a reference photo from your library for color sampling.
  • Photos Access (Add Only): Used solely when you choose to export and save an annotated swatch card image to your Camera Roll.

4. No Third-Party Trackers or Advertising

  • No User Tracking: The App contains no third-party tracking or behavioral analytics SDKs, does not request the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), and adheres strictly to Apple's NSPrivacyTracking = false manifest.
  • No Advertisements: There are no banner, interstitial, or third-party ads within the App.

5. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

  • All in-app purchases (Lifetime Pro and Pro Subscriptions) are processed directly through Apple's native StoreKit 2 framework.
  • Payment transactions, receipt validation, and subscription management are handled entirely by Apple. The developer does not collect, receive, or store your credit card or financial credentials.

6. Contact Us
